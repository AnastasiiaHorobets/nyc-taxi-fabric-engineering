# Analytical Findings

These are the main conclusions from the report. The values were checked on 2026-11-25.

## Taxi trips and air quality

Daily Taxi trips have a clear weekly pattern. PM2.5 and NO2 have more irregular peaks. Some pollution peaks happen on busy Taxi days, but the chart does not show a stable relationship for the whole period. It is therefore better to describe this as a date-based comparison, not proof that Taxi trips caused the pollution.

## Zones and locations

The Mobility page shows the busiest Taxi pickup zones. The Air Quality page shows the OpenAQ locations with the highest average PM2.5 values.

The two location types are different. OpenAQ stations were not mapped to TLC Taxi Zones, so the report does not compare a specific Taxi Zone with a specific sensor.

## Revenue per trip

The report showed:

- Average revenue per trip in USD: **29.56**
- Average revenue per trip in EUR: **25.84**

For the EUR calculation I used the latest ECB rate available on or before each trip date. This is needed for weekends and holidays.

## Longer-term comparison

GDP covers more years than Taxi and OpenAQ. The project gives useful economic context, but the common Taxi/Air Quality period is too short to make a strong long-term conclusion about economic growth and environmental quality.

## Conclusion

The report can be used to find dates and locations that may need a closer look. A stronger spatial or causal study would need station coordinates, a mapping to Taxi Zones, weather data and a longer common history.
